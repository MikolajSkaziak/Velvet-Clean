## Users

Homeowners in Kalisz and the surrounding area who own upholstered furniture worth looking after: sofas, corner sofas, armchairs, mattresses, rugs, car interiors. The site is pitched at affluent, design-conscious households ("ludzie z kasą i klasą"): people who bought good furniture and would rather restore it than replace it, often with children, pets or allergies at home. They arrive from Google Maps, Facebook or word of mouth, usually on a phone, and want to judge quickly whether this person can be trusted inside their home and with their furniture.

## Product Purpose

Velvet Clean is a one-person mobile upholstery cleaning service. The website turns a visitor into a call, a WhatsApp/Messenger message with a photo for a quote, or a booked visit. Success = more booked jobs from the target audience, not just more enquiries.

## Positioning

Extraction cleaning done in the client's home, with drying included in the price, free travel within Kalisz and surroundings, and certified products safe for children, allergy sufferers and animals. The furniture is usable again within a few hours. One craftsman, personally doing every job (Google reviews repeatedly mention "Pan" and his professionalism).

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: a stranger is letting someone into their home; proof (real results, real reviews, a real person) outranks adjectives.
2. Show the result, not the process chemistry: the visible difference is the product.
3. Contact in one tap from anywhere, phone first, photo-quote second.
4. Premium through restraint and care, never through claims the business cannot back.
