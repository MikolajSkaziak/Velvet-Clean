## Direction contract

THESIS: One pull restores the fabric. Scrolling draws a single gold cord across the black field and the sofa comes back clean behind it. Refuses the category default of a white page, a blue trust accent, stock family photos, and icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Matte black silk ground (#0b0b0d / #050506), channel graphite (#2b2e33) for hairline frames and dashed "channel stitch" rules, gather slate (#4a515b) for secondary text and quiet surfaces, ecru (#e9e4da) for reading text, drawcord gold (#c8a35a) reserved for the cord, the active state, and the one primary action per screen. Saira Extra Condensed set in tall tracked capitals for display and labels; Saira for reading copy. Frames are stitched rectangles, never rounded cards. Controls carry a cord toggle (a short gold line with a bead) at their trailing edge.

STORY: The visitor sees a dirty sofa in a dark studio, pulls the cord by scrolling, and watches it come back clean. Then come real results from Kalisz homes, a tailor-style price list, the route from photo to dry sofa, the reviews, and the phone number set as large as the headline.
